# Positions

What this project argues, kept apart from what it finds. A position is an argument built on claims; it is never presented as a finding. Each one names the claims that support it, the claims that cut against it, and what evidence would change it.

IDs are stable (`P-001`…). Don't delete; when a position changes, rewrite it and add a dated line under History.

| ID | Position | Rests on | Cuts against it | What would change it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| P-001 | Talent leaving to build is an output, not a leak. A research system should reward staff who leave to found or join startups, and measure how many do. | C-028, C-037, C-038, C-039; research/comparisons (ITRI, Nokia, Fraunhofer spin-off premium) | C-013 (centre spin-off rate on par with Fraunhofer) | Evidence that Basque centre alumni already found or lead startups at rates comparable to ITRI/Nokia benchmarks, or that releasing staff harms the centres' industrial role. |
| P-002 | Public money should accompany private money, not lead it: help at the beginning, then step aside. Its share of startup rounds should fall over time. | C-010, C-025, C-035; research/comparisons (Brander et al., Yozma) | C-027 (Basque VC per capita comparable to Madrid/Catalonia) | Evidence that Basque public-led rounds produce outcomes at least as good as private-led ones, or a fall in the public share without loss of deal flow. |
| P-003 | Public startup money must lead to a next step, and that next step must be named and tracked. If nobody tracks it, that absence is itself the finding. | C-031, C-032, C-036; research/public-funded-startups | C-032 (survival is decent for a pre-seed grant) | Publication by SPRI/GCR/Diputaciones of what became of the companies they funded. |
