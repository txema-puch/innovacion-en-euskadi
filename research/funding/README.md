# Public money flows: R&D programmes and startup funding

**Question.** Where does Basque public innovation money go, what are recipients rewarded for, and does anyone measure what it produces?

## 1. R&D programmes (Basque Government / SPRI)

| Programme | Who can receive | Size | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Hazitek** | Companies (RVCTI agents excluded as beneficiaries, Base 4.2.d) | 2024: €92.5M; 2025: €93M; 2026: €119M + €50M top-up (Apr 2026) = €169M | Co-financed by ERDF. Lines: competitive, strategic, "transformadores" (2026) |
| **Elkartek** | **Only accredited RVCTI agents** | 2025–26 call: €47M | Fundamental collaborative projects funded at **100%**. Firms take part only via an unfunded "comité empresarial" |
| **Emaitek / Emaitek Plus / Emaitek Industria Hobea** | Technology centres (structural, "non-economic activity") | €53.3M (2023); €58.6M (2025 call); €62.1M (2026) | Formula-based; grant capped at 27.5% of each centre's R&D income; scorecard below |
| **Azpitek** | Centres (equipment) | €26.7M over 2021–23 | |

**Hazitek steers business R&D to the centres (verified in the official rules).** Orden 16 dic 2025, BOPV nº250 (29/12/2025), https://www.euskadi.eus/bopv2/datos/2025/12/2505528a.pdf:
- Transformer projects (Base 6.1.c, ≥€20M, ≥3 firms): "Será necesaria la participación de, al menos, un agente de la RVCTI … como subcontratado … dicha participación, en su conjunto, será, como mínimo, del 20 % del total del presupuesto presentado."
- Strategic projects (Base 6.2.c.2, ≥€4M, ≥3 firms): same ≥20% clause. "Singular" strategic projects without it are capped at 20% of the strategic budget line.
- Competitive projects: no RVCTI requirement.
- A startup or other non-RVCTI firm **can** be subcontracted (Base 7.1.c) but **does not count toward the 20%**.
- No higher subsidy rate for RVCTI subcontracts (Base 9/17: industrial research 30–50%, experimental development 25%). The steering comes from eligibility, not price.
- Hazitek 2025 (Orden 23 dic 2024, BOPV nº253) had the same clause: https://www.euskadi.eus/bopv2/datos/2024/12/2406031a.pdf
- Back-of-envelope (estimate): €50M strategic grant at 25–50% intensity → €100–200M project budgets → ≥€20–40M forced to RVCTI agents.
- CDTI comparison: Misiones 2025 requires ≥15% subcontracting to research organisations.

**What the centres are scored on.** Emaitek 2026 scorecard (https://brta.euskadi.eus/news/2026/emaitek-industria-hobea-2026-eng/webbrta00-newsdet/en/): industrial impact 55% (SME transfer focus), technology leadership 25%, specialisation 10%, international 10%. **Spin-offs and startups are not a weighted indicator.** Open question: a ~2018 OECD case study written by Tecnalia says the Government paid centres partly on spin-off *revenues* over the last 5 years — dropped, or folded into "industrial impact"?

**Regional aid to centres (Ministry of Science evaluation, Nov 2025).** 2021–23: €254.76M to Basque centres (Emaitek Plus 62.5%, Elkartek 25.2%, Azpitek €26.7M). Average €18.82M per centre vs €3.1M in Galicia. Basque centres capture 43.5% of Spanish tech-centre Horizon Europe returns. **The Basque Country does not report to SICTI** (footnote 1).

## 2. Is "private" centre revenue really private?

- Tecnalia: "Sus ingresos en 2024 ascendieron a 147 millones, el 51% procedentes de la actividad con empresas" (HC-2025, p.1). "Actividad con empresas" is not defined; no split of grant-funded vs self-funded company contracts is published.
- **Ministry of Science evaluation (p.11):** "en muchos casos, estas contrataciones se realizan en el marco de proyectos ejecutados por empresas y financiados con ayudas públicas que incluyen subcontratación de parte del proyecto." Spain-wide company contracts with centres doubled 2019→2023 (€233.7M → €457.8M), attributed "al menos en parte" to more public funding of business R&D. 56.2% of centres' R&D spending was publicly funded in 2023.
- Precision: under Hazitek the company still pays 50–75% of the centre's fee from its own money. The circular share is the grant portion; the bigger effect is the eligibility rules steering R&D to centres.
- BRTA aggregate (own memorias): 2024 R&D revenue €367M = €154M private (42%) / €214M public (58%); 4,372 people.

## 3. Does anyone measure outcomes?

**No evaluation found that measures the market outcome of Hazitek, Elkartek or Emaitek projects** (share reaching market, revenue generated, additionality).
- Hazitek reporting ends at project close (technical-economic report). No post-project commercialisation follow-up; the 5-year obligation is only to keep the activity in the CAV. Market potential is scored only ex ante (20 pts, "plan de explotación").
- TVCP/HKEE (Basque audit court) publishes legality/financial audits, not performance audits of these programmes.
- AIReF's Spending Review phase III (launched Oct 2026) covers the state multi-regional ERDF programme, not the Basque one.
- Orkestra (Gipuzkoa R&D system, 2021): business R&D 21% publicly funded (EU-27 5%, Germany 3%); R&D services 42% of business R&D (EU-27 4%); patents per business researcher ~3x below Spain, ~6x below Germany, falling since 2008 — "clear inability to transform R&D activities into technological advances recognised internationally." Recommends assessing additionality and the "possible negative impact on business R&D culture." https://www.orkestra.deusto.es/images/investigacion/publicaciones/informes/cuadernos-orkestra/210027_Sistema_ID_Gipuzkoa_EXECUTIVE_SUMMARY.pdf
- Tecnalia's impact claims (€914M GDP, €34.10 per €1, "+€17.08 per €1 a company invests") come from commissioned consultancy studies (Deloitte, IDEA) with no published methodology; the figures are typical of input-output models (spending counted as impact), not measured outcomes against a counterfactual.

- Plus entrepreneurship tax expenditures: €56M (2024), €59M (2023).
- Apparatus: 156 agents (52 public), 793 actions 2025–28, ~800 programmes, 100+ public incubators/nurseries/coworkings. Outcomes: 46 scale-ups, 1 unicorn; startup valuation €2.0B (2023) → €1.9B (2024); BIND 4.0 contracts 53 (2019) → 24 (2024).
- The plan's own diagnosis: "atomización de agentes de apoyo"; the route is "confuso por el número de agentes y 'puertas de entrada'".
- Gipuzkoa 2024: "23M€ de fondos públicos y 3,95M€ privados" (85% public).
- **Open:** does UP! Euskadi's "public capital in rounds" include public loans (ENISA, CDTI, convertibles)? Verify before quoting the ratio externally.

**Benchmarks:** government agencies were 39% of European VC *fundraising* in 2025 (Invest Europe — fund level, not rounds); Israel public ≈3–5% of capital raised (estimate); US SBIR ≈2–3% of VC (estimate); Spain's public role is mostly fund-of-funds LP (Fond-ICO).

**Reading.** Public money in early rounds can fill a real gap (Howell 2017: a ~$150k SBIR Phase I doubles the odds of later VC). The problem is the trend: a catalyst should shrink as private money arrives (Yozma's state stakes were bought out within ~5 years). In Euskadi the public share rose from 44% (2017) to 60–85% (2018–2024). Brander, Du & Hellmann (2015): moderate government VC alongside private improves exits; heavy government VC worsens them.

## 5. Startup funding totals (context)

- El Referente: €85M in 2024 (36 rounds); €550M+ since 2016. Scoutyn/Basque Gov: 895 tech firms raised €324M in 2025 (10.09% of Spain), driven by Multiverse's single round.
- VC per capita 2025 (estimates, mixed sources): Euskadi ≈€147 (≈€61 without Multiverse), Madrid ≈€165, Catalonia ≈€147, US ≈$1,000, Bay Area ≈$23,000. Unicorns per million: Euskadi ≈0.45, Spain ≈0.3, US ≈6, Bay Area ≈88.
