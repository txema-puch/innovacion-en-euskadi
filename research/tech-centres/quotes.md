## 1. OECD STIP case study no. 18 — "Spin-offs support at Tecnalia"

- URL: https://stip.oecd.org/assets/TKKT/CaseStudies/18.pdf (downloaded 2026-10-23; byte-identical to brta/dl/oecd18.pdf, 978,685 bytes, 13 pages)
- Full title (p.1-2): "Spin-offs support at Tecnalia, Spain - Transforming technology into GDP: Case study contribution to the OECD TIP Knowledge Transfer and Policies project"
- Author (p.2): "Author: Asier Rufino, CEO Tecnalia Ventures"
- Date: the document itself carries no printed date. PDF metadata: CreationDate 27 March 2019 (Microsoft Word 2016; metadata Author field "RAVELLI Greta", i.e. the OECD-side file author, not the content author). Internal evidence consistent with early 2019: "In 2018 two new spin-offs were created, and one in 2017" (p.10). Treat as "OECD TIP case study, 2019".

### (a) Public fund to acquire RTO stakes — p.12, section "4. Interactions with broader national context", last bullet of the list of problems "beyond crossing valley of death / funding schemes":

> "Developing a public fund to acquire stakes from the RTOs in the context of spin-offs that are already selling / consolidated and where forcing liquidity events for shareholders could cause trouble in the spin-off otherwise."

Note: this is framed as a policy *wish/need* raised by Tecnalia Ventures, not as an existing fund.

### (b) Basque Government funding tied to spin-off revenues — p.12, same section:

> "We get funding from Basque Government based on impact outcomes on a wide array of indicators, spin-offs are one of them. In this context, we don´t get any money just for the sheer fact of creating a spin-off but rather for the amount of revenues generated by the spin-offs that we have created over the last 5 years. This results on a strong focus on high impact spin-offs."

### Other relevant quotes from the same document

- p.10: "Currently, there are 14 spin-off companies in the portfolio of Tecnalia Ventures. In 2018 two new spin-offs were created, and one in 2017."
- p.11: "In 2015, TECNALIA sold to GAMESA its participation in the shareholding of NEM Solutions company which has a turnover of 4.2M € and employs 41 people." (no price disclosed)
- p.11: "Key figures on OMEGA Incubation Acceleration Program (2013 – 2017): >350 business opportunities presented; On average at any time circa 40 business opportunities are in the accelerator"
- p.5: "TECNALIA Ventures is a subsidiary of TECNALIA that was set up in 2013 ..."; "With more than 20 employees, TECNALIA Ventures has also developed a consulting arm ..."

## 2. Tecnalia spin-off targets over time (primary: Tecnalia press releases on cms.tecnalia.com)

### 2021–2024 Strategic Plan — "5 startups a year"
- URL: https://cms.tecnalia.com/uploads/2021/09/NPTECNALIAPlanEstrategico2024_SEP21.pdf — dated "Bilbao, 30 de septiembre de 2021"
> "Cuantitativamente, el objetivo es crecer de forma inteligente para incrementar su actividad en un 26% hasta alcanzar los 140 millones de euros en 2024, consolidar un equipo de 1.525 personas, crear una media de 5 startups al año y alcanzar unos ingresos por venta de propiedad intelectual de hasta 8 millones de euros."
- Repeated in Balance 2021 release, https://cms.tecnalia.com/uploads/2022/07/NPTECNALIABalance2021Proyecto2022-1.pdf (6 July 2022): "... consolidar un equipo de 1.525 personas, crear una media de 5 startups al año ..."

### 2025–2027 Strategic Plan — €25M revenue, >220 people, €250M value by 2027
- URL: https://cms.tecnalia.com/uploads/2024/12/NPTECNALIAPlanEstrategico2025-2027_DIC2024.docx — dated "Bilbao, 12 de diciembre de 2024"
> "Así, el reto es, además de crear nuevas startups, duplicar tanto la facturación, como la creación de empleo y el valor de las empresas generadas por TECNALIA. Por ello, la previsión es alcanzar los 25 millones de euros en ingresos para 2027, con un equipo de más de 220 personas e incrementar el valor de estas startups hasta los 250 millones de euros."
> "Además, TECNALIA a lo largo de este último Plan Estratégico ha impulsado el crecimiento de startups, como H2Site, ..., Fesia, ..., Crisalion, ..., o Neos Surgery, ..."
> President Alex Belaustegui on the 2021-24 plan: "era ambicioso, pero hemos cumplido los objetivos con creces, a nivel cualitativo y cuantitativo." (no startup count given)

## 4. Specific verifications (lead's own checks)
- **Biotech Foods / JBS** — nanoGUNE press release 18/11/2021, https://nanogune.eu/es/nanogune/sala-prensa/gigante-brasileno-jbs-invierte-36-millones-productor-vasco-carne-cultivada: "La inversión de 36 millones realizada por el gigante brasileño JBS" ; "Todos los socios actuales, incluida nanoGUNE, siguen siendo accionistas de la empresa." Majority (~51%) per Cuatrecasas (https://www.cuatrecasas.com/es/spain/mercantil/art/la-multinacional-jbs-invierte-36-millones-en-biotech-foods-via-ampliacion-de-capital-1) and BORME capital increase +104% inscribed 13/05/2022 (empresia.es). Not a full acquisition; no valuation disclosed.
- **Smowltech** — Vicomtech's site claims "Exit in 2025". Registry (https://www.empresia.es/empresa/smiley-owl-tech/): "la sociedad pasa de tener 127.850€ de capital social a 117.850€. Esta reducción de capital fue inscrita en el Registro Mercantil con fecha 19 de marzo de 2025" and a further €5,263 reduction inscribed 11/12/2025; VESMO LEGACY CAPITAL SL appointed Presidente 23/02/2026. Consistent with a share buyback/amortisation of a departing shareholder; no buyer or price published. Company still independent (32 employees per empresia, 56 on LinkedIn).
- **H2SITE** — no evidence of any Tecnalia share sale; Tecnalia participated in the 2022 €12.5M Series A (BEV lead) and is not named in the 2025 €36M Series B (Hy24 + SC Net Zero Ventures). Stake % never disclosed. See https://www.tecnalia.com/en/news/h2site-leads-the-energy-transition-after-closing-the-latest-investment-round and https://siliconcanals.com/h2site-raises-12-5m/.
- **NEM Solutions** — sale to Gamesa: Tecnalia's 15% + CAF's 35% (agreed Dec 2015, closed May 2016); price not disclosed in any source found. OECD gives €4.2M turnover, 41 people.
