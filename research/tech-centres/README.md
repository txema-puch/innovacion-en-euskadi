- **Targets vs delivery:** 2021–24 plan targeted ~5 startups/year; delivered 2–3. 2027 targets: portfolio value €250M, portfolio revenue €25M (≈3x in two years), >220 people (target source is secondary — verify).
- **Exits:** only one documented: NEM Solutions stake sold to Gamesa (2015; NEM then had €4.2M revenue, 41 staff; price undisclosed). Partial liquidity: Subsea 7 bought 59.12% of Nautilus Floating Solutions (2021), Tecnalia kept 29.14%. No write-offs published.
- **Real successes held (not exits):** H2SITE (2020; €12.5M Series A 2022 led by Breakthrough Energy Ventures; €36M Series B Jan 2025), NEOS Surgery, Ingredalia.
- **The exit problem in its own words** (OECD STIP case study written by Tecnalia, ~2018, https://stip.oecd.org/assets/TKKT/CaseStudies/18.pdf): asks for "a public fund to acquire stakes from the RTOs" in consolidated spin-offs. Same document: the Government funds the centre partly "not for the sheer fact of creating a spin-off but for the revenues generated by the spin-offs created over the last 5 years." *(Verbatim check in progress.)*
- **Rate vs Fraunhofer:** 3 spin-offs / 1,512 staff ≈ 2.0 per 1,000 staff/yr, on par with Fraunhofer (1.57 per 1,000 total staff; 2.8 per 1,000 scientific staff; Bundestag Drs. 19/14361). **The weakness is outcomes, not count.**

## Basque Tek Ventures (BTV)

- **What:** SPRI-led venture builder for centre technology, with BRTA, GCR (Gestión de Capital Riesgo del País Vasco) and the BICs. Vehicle: BasketekVenture FCR (managed by GCR).
- **Since:** first call Feb–Mar 2023; second call Mar–Apr 2024; 2026 edition opened to BERCs. **No fund size or budget published.**
- **Terms (official call rules, both editions):** up to €250k per startup in tranches, equity or participative loan convertible "when BTV decides"; BTV can stop disbursing; ~€1M pre-money max; centre grants exclusive licence with 1–5% royalties; indicative cap table **centre <50%, BTV <40%**, founding team a "significant" stake conditional on milestones; during venture building **BTV decides the type and amount of every expense, the suppliers, any debt**. CEO recruited externally (job ads: "from age 30, sales experience"). Sources: `sources/txt/btv1.txt`, `btv2.txt`, `ehu2.txt`.
- **Reported results:** Feb 2024: 20 proposals / 11 selected / 4 approved. Jan 2025: 36 projects submitted, 8–9 companies incorporated, BTV invested in 7. No names or amounts; no update since.
- **Known companies:** Orbik (Ikerlan; active, strongest; became Mondragon–Ikerlan mixed cooperative 2025), InspectRail (CEIT; €1.5M round Sept 2026 led by GCR via BTV), Optidel (GAIKER; last news Mar 2025). BideRoad (CEIT), MELISA (biomaGUNE), Detector BMCP (nanoGUNE), NEWRO (Tecnalia): looking for a CEO in Dec 2023; status unknown.
