# Talent: does the system turn trained people into builders, or keep them?

**Question.** Basque tech centres, CICs/BERCs and universities train thousands of researchers and engineers. Does the system turn them into founders and startup builders, or keep them?

**Position (not a finding):** talent leaving to build is an output, not a leak. A centre that cared about impact would measure how many of its alumni found companies or lead tech at startups.

## Work plan

1. **Rules and incentives:** what centre, university and public-employee rules say about leaving to found (excedencias, return rights, IP/licence terms for founders, researcher equity, any spin-off premium like Fraunhofer's). Primary documents only.
2. **Alumni count (C-028 gap):** ex-staff of Tecnalia, CEIT, Ikerlan, Vicomtech who founded or hold founder/CTO/early roles at startups, from public sources only. Reverse check: where the founders in `../tech-centres/spinoffs.csv` and `../public-funded-startups/startups.csv` came from.
3. **Benchmarks in the same unit:** ITRI turnover and alumni, ex-Nokia (ETLA), Fraunhofer/VTT alumni founders, imec.
4. **Does anyone measure it?** BRTA memorias, PCTI 2030 indicators, PIE 2025-28 panel. A documented absence is a fact.

## Personal data

Aggregate counts in the public repo. Per-person rows only for people publicly presented as founders (company site or press), with the URL. Everything else stays in `raw/` (gitignored). No private profile details; no automated LinkedIn scraping.
