# Method: census extension, Ekintzaile 2022–2025 and Barne-Ekintzaile 2022–2024

Extends the pilot (`~/Claude/innovacion-en-euskadi/research/public-funded-startups/`, Ekintzaile 2021) with the same field rules, plus five new columns. Outcomes observed 2026-10-23.

## Cohort rule

| Call | Source of the legal-entity list | Source of the natural-person tally |
|---|---|---|
| Ekintzaile 2022 | BDNS call 787614 (65 awards, all with CIF) | BOPV n.º 238, 09/12/2024 (`2405627a`, 100 rows) |
| Ekintzaile 2023 | BDNS call 772515 (57 awards) + 1 BOPV-only company (Olive Robotics) | BOPV n.º 97, 26/05/2025 (`2502255a`, 94 rows) |
| Ekintzaile 2024 | BDNS call 769097 (88 awards) | BOPV n.º 102, 02/06/2025 (`2502427a`, 122 rows) |
| Ekintzaile 2025 | BOPV n.º 156, 18/08/2026 (`2603645a`, 132 rows): rows with a legal suffix, plus 2 suffix-less brand names that resolve to companies | same PDF |
| Barne-Ekintzaile 2022/2023/2024 | BDNS calls 783079 (31), 772453 (39), 769096 (36) | n/a (BDNS has no natural persons for Barne) |

PDF URL pattern: `https://www.euskadi.eus/bopv2/datos/<yyyy>/<mm>/<code>.pdf`. BDNS: `https://www.infosubvenciones.es/bdnstrans/api/concesiones/busqueda?vpd=GE&numeroConvocatoria=<n>&pageSize=500`.

- **BDNS publishes only legal entities** for these calls; the BOPV list publishes everyone. For 2022–24 the legal-entity cohort is the BDNS list, matched to BOPV file numbers by fuzzy name + amount. Unmatched BOPV rows are natural persons (counted, never named).
- **Promoter-to-company conversions.** 1 BDNS company in 2022 and 18 in 2024 do not appear in BOPV under their own name. BOPV lists the natural-person promoter; BDNS records the company the promoter later formed, with its CIF. These companies are in the cohort (no BOPV file number), and the corresponding BOPV person rows are still counted in the natural-person tally, so **person tallies and company tallies overlap for those 19** (€360k). The BDNS record is therefore the only public promoter→company link, and only for 2022 and 2024.
- **Excluded:** comunidades de bienes (CB, CIF letter E: one in Ekintzaile 2023 €22.5k, one in Barne 2024 €7.5k). They have no legal personality and their names are personal names; counted with natural persons.
- **Kept but outside BORME:** cooperatives (F), associations/foundations (G), sociedad civil (J). Status left unknown unless a primary source says otherwise.
- **Ekintzaile+ (2024: 10, 2025: 11 companies, up to €100k each) is out of scope** and not in the CSV.
- **Barne-Ekintzaile is not a startup programme in practice.** It funds intrapreneurship inside existing firms; most beneficiaries are incumbents (Deportivo Alavés, ITP, Sidenor, Fagor Ederlan, Lantek, tech centres). Rows are kept as one row per award (Lortek, Ideko and Vicomtech received two awards each); the `notes` flag "incumbent parent" where the entity was ≥4 years old at award. Their classification describes the parent, not the funded project.
- **Correction to the pilot's `sources.md`:** `2603645a` is the **Ekintzaile 2025** list, not Barne-Ekintzaile; `2603646a` is Ekintzaile+ 2025. Barne-Ekintzaile resolutions were not located in BOPV (BDNS suffices for legal entities).

## Field rules (new or changed vs pilot)

- **cif:** BDNS for 2022–24; infoempresa (`empresaCif` in page) for 2025.
- **Identity checks.** infoempresa slug pages are accepted only if their CIF equals the BDNS CIF (tries `-sl`, `-slu`, `-sll`, `-sa`). empresia pages are found by slug, then by empresia's own name search (`/busqueda/?q=`), and are rejected if the registered name differs from the grant name *and* the incorporation date differs from infoempresa's. Rejections are written in `notes`.
- **status:** as pilot (empresia ACTIVA/EXTINGUIDA/DISUELTA/CIERRE HOJA REGISTRAL → active/extinct/dissolved/dormant; infoempresa fallback).
- **last_registry_act_date / type:** most recent BORME act on the company's empresia page (inscription date). Acts that empresia shows because the company appears in *another* company's filing ("Publicación de la empresa X") are excluded.
- **capital_increases:** count from empresia's act summary, then the dates of those shown (`N (dd/mm/yyyy; …)`). Nominal amounts exclude share premium, so they are not round sizes.
- **employees / revenue_eur:** infoempresa ranges, active companies only. **revenue_year** = latest fiscal year of annual accounts listed as available at infoempresa (`availableYears`); the ranges are not tied to a year by the source.
- **website_status:** `live` = a guessed domain (`<name>`, `<first word>`, `<first two words>`, hyphenated; .com/.es/.io/.eus/.ai/.tech) returns 200, is not a parked/for-sale/placeholder page, and contains the company name. `dead` = only set from manual evidence (parked/for-sale domain that the company demonstrably used). `none` = nothing found; it does **not** mean the company has no website (guessing misses brand names that differ from the legal name). Last activity = latest © year on the page, given in `notes`.
- **sector:** keyword rules over the registered objeto social (+ name); `unknown` when nothing matched. Coarse.
- **classification** (automatic, then manually overridden where noted in `notes`):
  - **DEAD** (added to the requested taxonomy): status extinct/dissolved/dormant.
  - **ABSORBED:** acquired or merged, from a primary source (BORME merger act, company website).
  - **GROWING:** active and, in the last 24 months, a capital increase (funding), an employee range above 1–9 (hiring), a revenue range above €2M, or manual evidence (plant, customers, round) cited in `notes`.
  - Capital increases within 6 months of incorporation are treated as founding capitalisation, not growth.
  - **OPERATING:** active, not GROWING, and at least one sign of life: a BORME act (beyond the incorporation batch) in the last 36 months, a live website with © ≥ 2024 (or no © shown), or annual accounts on file for FY2024 or later. Accounts count because a stable small firm can go years without any BORME act. Shells also file accounts, so OPERATING is a generous class.
  - **ZOMBIE:** active, no sign of life as above, and incorporated ≥24 months ago (≥36 months if infoempresa data is missing); or active with an insolvency (concurso) act in the last 36 months.
  - **UNCLEAR:** status unknown (cooperatives, foundations, unresolved names) or too young / too little data to judge.

## Limitations

1. Outcome window is short: 1–4 years for Ekintzaile 2022–2025. "Active" for a 2025 company says almost nothing.
2. Funding rounds unknown (no web search). Capital increases are a weak proxy.
3. Website discovery by domain guessing has many misses; GROWING via web evidence was checked only for a short list.
4. infoempresa ranges are very coarse ("1–9 employees", "<€2M").
5. The 2025 cohort has no CIF from an official source; CIF comes from infoempresa slug match, so a name collision is possible where `notes` show no constitution-date cross-check.

## Reproducing

Scripts in `scripts/` (run with `python3 -I`); downloads in `dl/` (untrusted).
1. `parse_bopv.py bopv.json dl/bopv/*.txt` (after `pdftotext -layout`).
2. `cohort.py <W>` → `cohort.json`, `persons.json`.
3. `fetch2.py cohort.json dl/co em|ie` (empresia / infoempresa).
4. `parse2.py cohort.json dl/co parsed.json`.
5. `webcands.py` + `probe.sh` → `web_hits.txt`; `webcheck.py` → `webcheck.json`.
6. `manual.json` holds manual overrides with their source URL; `build2.py <W>` → `startups_2022_2025.csv`.

## Access gap
infoempresa.com started returning HTTP 403 to this machine (all pages, including via the user's Chrome) after ~380 companies; ~35 rows lack employee/revenue ranges and accounts year. A later re-run of `retry.py` would fill them.
