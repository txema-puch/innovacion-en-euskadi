## Problems hit and how they were handled
- **BDNS↔BOPV name matching.** A fuzzy match with an amount bonus wrongly paired AIDTEC Solutions (B70893615) with the BOPV row for Qlarix Healthcare Solutions. I raised the threshold to name similarity ≥0.75, which leaves one wrong pair fixed and no other match below 0.82.
- **BOPV PDF typos.** For example "AddresS.L.ess For Send", "Bloodhiund", "Technologiers", "S.L..u". These were resolved with BDNS names and manual alternates (`alt.json`).
- **infoempresa entries with empty CIF.** For recent companies infoempresa shows no CIF. I accepted the slug page when the CIF is empty and the name matches. The 3 real CIF mismatches were rejected.
- **empresia mismatches.** Same-name pages belonging to different entities (Fresmak 2025 vs 1983, and others) are caught by the incorporation-date cross-check, which has a 120-day tolerance and 2 years for identical names. 49 empresia pages carry a CIF in the title and are CIF-verified.
- **empresia indirect acts.** empresia lists acts of *other* companies that mention the target ("Publicación de la empresa X"). These are excluded unless X is the company itself.
- **Owner field.** The sole-shareholder field is reproduced only when it is a company. Natural-person owners are not named.
