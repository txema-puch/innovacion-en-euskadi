# Trigger study v2 — "how often is the administration the news?" (log)

Coded 2026-10-23. Period 2025-10-01 → 2026-09-30. Codebook v1 (`../codebook.md`) plus the v2 trigger taxonomy requested for this study.
Outputs: `articles.csv` (143 articles, one row each), `speakers.csv` (codebook press format, 291 rows), this log. Headline numbers: `summary.txt` (generated by `tools/analyze.py`).

## 1. Design and what changed from v1

The aim was to control for outlet type. Only the main general dailies were used, in their local or business sections:

- **EUSKADI:** El Correo, El Diario Vasco, Deia (whole domain).
- **MADRID:** El Mundo (Madrid), El País (Madrid), ABC (Madrid), Madridiario, 20minutos (Madrid).
- **BAYAREA:** San Francisco Chronicle, SF Standard, Mercury News, KQED.

**Discovery.** Google News RSS. Each run used one site filter, one query and one calendar-month window (`after:YYYY-MM-01 before:next-month`).
- Spanish queries: `startup`, `startups`, `emprendimiento`, `emprendedores` (hl=es, gl=ES).
- English queries: `startup`, `founders` (hl=en-US, gl=US).
- Totals: 480 RSS files, in `rss/`. Two transient errors were retried successfully.
- Scripts: `tools/fetch_rss.py` and `tools/build_cands.py`.

**Candidate order (EUSKADI, BAYAREA).** Within each month, queries were taken in order, and within a query, rank by rank, round-robin across sites. Results were deduplicated by title.
- Files: `cands_eus.json` and `cands_ba.json`.
- EUSKADI candidate pool: 795. BAYAREA candidate pool: 3,399. The `founders` queries were never reached because the `startup` results filled every month.

**MADRID deviation (pass B).** Path-restricted `site:` filters (for example `site:elmundo.es/madrid`) returned very thin pools, 3–22 hits a month. A supplementary pass was added (`tools/fetch_rss_mad2.py`, saved in `rss_mad2/`):
- It used domain-level `site:` with the queries `startup Madrid`, `startups Madrid`, `emprendimiento Madrid` and `emprendedores Madrid`.
- Ordering in `cands_mad.json`: pass A (path-restricted) first, then pass B.
- Pass B is ordered rank-major across all query × site combinations, because its pool is about 500 a month. The first version was query-major; `cands_mad_old.json` reproduces that order and maps the page IDs `M####`. New IDs are `N####`.
- In pass B, an article was eligible only if its text places the startup or actor in the Comunidad de Madrid. Cinco Días hits, which come in through `site:elpais.com`, and supplements (Sapos y Princesas, Yo Dona) were discarded as outside the outlet frame.

**Cap.** At most 5 eligible articles per region-month, taken in candidate order. No month was topped up from another.

**Final sample.** EUSKADI 48, MADRID 36, BAYAREA 59. The target was 60 per region; see §5 for the shortfalls.

## 2. Access, and how each article was read

**URL resolution.** Google News `/articles/` links returned Google's `sorry` (rate-limit) page to curl.
- Links were therefore decoded inside the user's Chrome. A news.google.com tab ran in-page `fetch` calls to the same RSS search and to Google's `batchexecute` decoder. The call included the page's own xsrf token (`at`), which the page uses for its own redirect.
- This was read-only, with no form submissions or clicks.
- Resolved URLs are in `urls.tsv`.

**Page fetch.** Pages were fetched with `curl` (`tools/get.sh`), stored in `pages/` and extracted to `txt/`. All downloaded content was treated as untrusted; Python ran with `-I`.

**Read in Chrome** (approved fallback; read-only, in a new tab):
- E48, El Correo: readable in Chrome but not via curl.
- Paywalled in Chrome too, so not coded: E51, E57 (El Correo premium) and E99, E104, E129 (Diario Vasco premium).
- El País returned a bot-protection page both to curl and in Chrome. Nothing was clicked and no CAPTCHA was attempted.

**Read via Wayback Machine** (El País only): N31, N575, N1626, and the excluded M34, M558, M566, N53, N573. M2992 and N2527 returned 403 on Wayback and are logged as unreadable.

**Vocento paywall.** El Correo and Diario Vasco "premium" pages serve only the headline and subhead, sometimes a few later paragraphs. 63 EUSKADI candidates were lost this way; their titles are logged in `screen.tsv` with the trigger each title implies.
- One coded EUSKADI article is partial, with the closing part missing: E669.
- Publisher AMP URLs (`-nt_amp.html`) serve the full text. They were **not** used, because that would be paywall circumvention. If you want this, it is a decision for you.

**Partial pages coded.** Where only the opening (or closing) paragraph was missing, the article was coded from the visible text and flagged `PARTIAL` in notes: M21, M4535, N3448, N4534, E669.

## 3. Inclusion rules (applied identically)

An article was included if it is primarily about:
- a startup or young tech venture in the region, or
- the regional startup, entrepreneurship or tech-ecosystem support system: programmes, funds, incubators, awards, events, reports, ecosystem policy.

Excluded:
- single traditional small businesses (shops, bars, restaurants, consumer brands with no venture profile);
- general business or corporate awards where startups are a minority (E56, E174, E196, M5646);
- industrial or real-estate infrastructure that is not startup-specific (E466 tech-park expansion, B252 East Bay manufacturing, B2501 office market, B2821);
- school or student education programmes (STARTInnova, Mondragon City Challenge, bootcamps);
- startups outside the region, or whose location the text does not state (v1 rule), for example the Valencian, Barcelona and Navarra B-Venture participants;
- gossip columns (The Waggle), opinion and editorials;
- crime stories that are personal rather than company news (B697).

**`scope` column.** STARTUP (a venture-type startup or the startup ecosystem), ENTREPRENEURSHIP (general entrepreneurship support: young farmers, commerce, self-employed, business transfer) or TECH_ECOSYSTEM. EUSKADI and MADRID include ENTREPRENEURSHIP pieces because the `emprendimiento/emprendedores` queries surface them. Results are therefore reported both on all articles and on STARTUP scope only.

Every screening decision (exclude, paywall, beyond_cap, unreadable) is in `screen.tsv`, by region, candidate index and month.

## 4. Coding definitions and conventions

### trigger_v2

What generated the article:
- **STARTUP_MILESTONE** is used broadly for any company-generated event: round, launch, customer or pilot, hire, exit, lease, relocation, layoffs, incident, controversy.
- **STARTUP_PROFILE** covers features and interviews.
- **PUBLIC_\*** and **OFFICIAL_VISIT** require a public body as organiser or announcer. Branded content paid for by a public body counts.
- **OTHER** covers trend features, court or regulatory enforcement, private-association programmes (there is no PRIVATE_PROGRAMME code), and cases where the organiser is not stated.

**institution_is_news** is `yes` if trigger_v2 is PUBLIC_\* or OFFICIAL_VISIT.

**Ambiguous calls** (coded conservatively, with the alternative flagged in notes):

| Article | Coded as | Why it is ambiguous |
|---|---|---|
| E115 | OTHER | Award; organiser not named |
| E506 | OTHER | Music-entrepreneurship programme run by an association with public funding |
| E669 | POLICY_DEBATE | Officials-led AI feature |
| M2494, M3972 | STARTUP_* | Startups framed through the Comunidad's RETECH programme |
| N4534 | STARTUP_MILESTONE | Municipal pilot |

`summary.txt` reports the share with all of these flipped to PUBLIC_\*.

### startup_role

- **PROTAGONIST:** a specific startup, or startups collectively, are the subject.
- **PROP:** named startups appear as example, beneficiary, participant or data source while the subject is something else.
- **ABSENT:** no startup named.

### Count and actor fields

- `named_startups_count` and `named_officials_count` count names appearing anywhere in the text, including photo captions.
- `first_paragraph_actor` and `headline_actor` use the speaker categories. A startup or its founder maps to FOUNDER. A generic actor (for example "las startups") maps to NONE. A public body maps to PUBLIC_OFFICIAL.
- `lead_voice` is the first coded speaker.

### Speakers

Codebook categories, with the v1 conventions:
- Founder status is taken from general knowledge only for well-known founders, and flagged in notes. Otherwise a CEO is coded STARTUP_EMP.
- Joint attributions are coded per person, with the note "joint".
- Officials presented as announcing, and on-stage speakers who are not quoted, are coded with a note. The "strict" variant drops them, along with joint-only attributions and historical epigraphs.
- The outlet's own hosts and reporters, unnamed sources, and attendee lists are not coded.
- First-name-only speakers are coded OTHER.
- Public-university staff are PUBLIC_ADJACENT. Private-university staff are PRIVATE_ECOSYSTEM.
- YC and a16z programme staff are PRIVATE_ECOSYSTEM. Bank and real-estate executives are CORPORATE.

### pr flag

`pr=yes` is a judgement call when the article:
- carries an institution or company byline or photo credit (for example DFB, Irekia, Ayuntamiento Durango, Cecobi);
- is labelled branded or paid content (20minutos "Contenido de marca … Alayans Studio para Ayuntamiento de Madrid");
- sits in a press-release section (Madridiario "notas de prensa"); or
- has a byline of only the outlet name plus a release structure.

Wire copy (EFE, Europa Press, Bloomberg) is `pr=no`.

## 6. Caveats

1. **EUSKADI is undersampled through the paywall, and the gap is not neutral.** 63 eligible-looking candidates were premium and unreadable. 42 of them have a title-implied trigger, and 17 of those 42 (40%) are PUBLIC_\* (programme 10, fund 3, award 2, report 1, event 1). Pooling the read articles with those titles gives roughly (12+17)/(48+42) = 32% for EUSKADI. The 25% headline is therefore probably a lower bound. Premium also falls heavily on El Correo's "talento-negocios" startup features and on Diario Vasco's January supplement on BIC Gipuzkoa.
2. **The B-Venture effect in EUSKADI.** El Correo's own event, B-Venture, generates a series of startup profiles (8 coded) and PRIVATE_EVENT items. That raises the PROTAGONIST and PRIVATE shares. Several profiles were excluded because the startup is not Basque.
3. **MADRID's local frame is institution-dominated; its national sections are not.**
   - Madrid local sections (El Mundo/ABC/20minutos Madrid and Madridiario): 19/27 (70%) institution-news. National business sections: 1/9 (11%).
   - The path-filtered pools were thin. Most domain-level hits were national stories or stories from other regions, which were excluded under the location rule. Hence n=36, with December at 0 and January, May and August thin.
   - 3 of the 20 MADRID institution-news items are paid municipal branded content. 4 more are release-style.
   - El País was largely unreadable (bot protection). Only 3 El País articles are in the sample, via Wayback.
4. **The BAYAREA "startup" frame includes large AI labs** (OpenAI, Anthropic, Cursor, Cognition), as v1 did. STARTUP_MILESTONE includes leases, layoffs and controversies. Federal and city actors appear mainly as context. The 3 institution-news items:
   - B1270: Figure robot at a White House event;
   - B1946: congressman's event on visas;
   - B2825: city chief economist's assessment.
   KQED Forum items are partial transcripts.
5. **Inclusion is the biggest lever on the metric.** In EUSKADI and MADRID, much institution-news is general "emprendimiento" support (farmers, commerce, self-employed, business transfer). Restricting to STARTUP scope roughly halves the share (EUSKADI 17%, MADRID 36%). The BAYAREA queries surface almost no equivalent coverage.
6. **Discovery rank.** The sampling frame is Google News relevance rank. In BAYAREA the `startup` query alone filled every month, so `founders` was never reached. The "returned order" rule differs slightly across regions: EUSKADI and BAYAREA query-major; MADRID pass B rank-major. See §1.
7. **Single coder, no inter-rater check.** About one article in five involved a judgement call, logged in notes. The pr flag is especially judgemental.
8. **No AMP or paywall circumvention was used.** Chrome was used for URL decoding (Google News) and for a handful of reads, as listed in §2.
